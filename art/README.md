# Artwork

**Art by Claude (Anthropic).** Every page of Loop-SaboteurXXL's skin is drawn by code in `tools/` (`look.py` the
shapes, `art.py` the pages, `skin.py` the skin MPC loads), designed by Claude after Loop Saboteur's own interface:
its colours (black `#1a1a1a`, orange `#ff5a3c`, white, p-lock yellow, MOD cyan), its knobs with a value arc, its
italic stencil headings, the orange CHANCE and white MIX strips and the Acts of Sabotage step cells. No pictures from
the original plugin are used, and no logos or wordmarks of any company.
